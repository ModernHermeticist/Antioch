# Strings patterns for Correspondence's pattern library. One family per line:
#   name | kind | moods | styles | gate | arp order | low | mid | high | fill [| fill 2 | high 2 | feel | colour | where] ("-" = none)
# feel: s = shuffle, or a share of a 16th. colour: 7, 9, i1, i2. where: only in I intro, G groove, K breakdown, B build, D drop, O outro.
# Bars: 16 steps, or 12 (8th triplets) or 24 (16th triplets). "!" before a step accents it, an apostrophe softens it. p/P = the next chord, early.
# moods: E euphoric, H hopeful, M melancholic, K dark, D driving, R dreamy, P epic (* = all)
# styles: U uplifting, C classic 138, P progressive, T tech, Y psy, V vocal, O orchestral (* = all)
# arp order: u up, d down, w up-down, c converge, s skip, - (none)
# Patterns: a character per 16th (see PatternLibrary.h), bars split by '/', voices by '+'.
# A voice starting with '<' or '>' sits an octave lower or higher. Spaces are ignored.
# Strings kinds: 0 legato, pads and lines (breakdowns, outros), 1 ostinato, spiccato, pizzicato, tremolo (builds, later drops).
# Voices: cellos and basses ('<'), violas and violins (chords), a violin line ('>').
